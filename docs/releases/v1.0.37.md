# WS Hub 1.0.37

What changed since 1.0.36. Hover tooltips on live lists now hold steady. A person still starts every transmit. Nothing in this release transmits or retunes a radio.

## Updates

**Fix: tooltips no longer flash.** Hover text on the Spotted list (SKIM, PSKR and CLU rows), the call roster, and the Status page tables (instances, loggers, decodes, antennas, band inventory, agents) used to flash on and off, because the list was redrawn every second under the mouse. While your mouse is over one of these lists, it now waits, and it catches up as soon as the mouse leaves. If you leave the mouse resting on one row, the list still refreshes every 8 seconds, so new stations are never held back for long. Moving to another row restarts that 8 seconds.

**Some things still update right away.** On the call roster, a station calling you, a row with Enable Tx set, and a change in whether you can transmit always show at once, even with the mouse over the list. Sorting, picking columns, and the band, needed, and age choices also redraw at once. On Spotted, the selected station's highlight follows your click straight away. Clicking a row works the same as before.

## Installing from this page

**Linux.** Download `wims-1.0.37-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and unpack it anywhere. Open the folder, open **scripts**, and double-click **Install WS Hub**. If the file manager asks once, choose **Allow Launching**, **Execute**, or **Run**. Then delete the download and double-click **WS Hub**.

**Windows.** Download `wims-1.0.37-windows-x86_64.zip` and extract it to `C:\WIMS`. Open `C:\WIMS\scripts\windows` and double-click **Install-Wims.cmd**. Then double-click **WS Hub**.
