# WS Hub 1.0.36

What changed since 1.0.35. Needed stations from PSK Reporter on Operate, a tidier Setup page, and a safeguard for IF-reporting seats. A person still starts every transmit. Nothing in this release transmits or retunes a radio.

## Updates

**PSK Reporter on the Spotted list.** Spotted now shows needed stations that PSK Reporter has with your grid square, with a blue **PSKR** tag. That means a station in your square heard them, or they heard a station in your square, in the last 15 minutes, so they are on the air now and the band is open toward them. They use the same needed check as the call roster. Hover the tag to see who heard whom and how strong. A **PSKR** checkbox sits beside **Skim** and **Cluster** to hide them. WS Hub uses the PSK Reporter lookup it already makes every five minutes, so this needs **Show spots** and **Use PSK Reporter to rank spots** turned on in Setup. Only the bands checked in Setup under DX cluster are shown.

**Needed by band.** A line above the Spotted list counts the needed stations on each band, for example `40M 2 on · 20M 2 · 10M 3`. **on** means a WSJT-X is already on that band. Hover a band to see how many came from WS Skim, PSK Reporter, and the cluster. Use it to decide whether to change bands.

**Spotted columns.** Spotted has its own **columns** menu with the same choices as the call roster, plus the tag, Spotter, and QSY. Your choice is kept in this browser.

**Setup is shorter.** The long paragraphs under **DX cluster** and **Skim receivers** are gone. The same help is now in the hover text on each box, and on the **?** beside each heading. The live status lines stay visible.

**IF-reporting seats.** The list in Setup no longer jumps while your mouse is over it, so a click lands on the seat you meant. A seat that is ticked but whose WSJT-X dial is not 21–24 MHz or 28–29.7 MHz now shows **⚠ dial is not an IF**. If you see that warning, untick that seat. Your Flex, your IC-9700, and WS Skim all report the real frequency and should not be ticked. Only a radio that reads its IF, such as a 28 MHz rig behind a 432 transverter, belongs on this list. While a seat is wrongly ticked, 10m and 15m on it are shown and logged as 70cm and 1.25m.

**Fix: phantom stations from testing.** Developer test runs could put fake WSJT-X stations (such as TRL-432 and VA2WXY on 432 MHz) on a running WS Hub on the same PC for a minute. Test tools now use their own private network address and can no longer reach a real WS Hub.

## Installing from this page

**Linux.** Download `wims-1.0.36-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` and unpack it anywhere. Open the folder, open **scripts**, and double-click **Install WS Hub**. If the file manager asks once, choose **Allow Launching**, **Execute**, or **Run**. Then delete the download and double-click **WS Hub**.

**Windows.** Download `wims-1.0.36-windows-x86_64.zip` and extract it to `C:\WIMS`. Open `C:\WIMS\scripts\windows` and double-click **Install-Wims.cmd**. Then double-click **WS Hub**.
